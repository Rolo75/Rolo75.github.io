What is Hopsie?

In Hopsie your apps don't sit still. Each one gets a body that fits what it does, with eyes, gloved arms and its real icon on its belly.

When a message arrives, its character hops up and tells you who wrote. Ignore it and it insists; keep ignoring it and it crosses its arms and ends up sitting down, resigned, until you open the app. At night they fall asleep, and the one with a notification wakes up to remind you.

They chat with each other in their own whistle language or with a real voice, with a grown-up or teen attitude. On phones with Gemini Nano they use the phone's own AI to sum up notifications and suggest replies.

The app has no internet permission: it can't send anything off your phone. Notification access is optional and explained before it's requested.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hopsie?

An Android home screen where every app is a character that reacts to its notifications, chats and helps you reply.

How much is it and when is it out?

€3.99, paid once, no subscriptions or in-app purchases. It goes through a closed test on Google Play first; if you'd like to try it for free, write to us.

Does it need internet or collect data?

No. The app has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.

Do the AI features work on my phone?

Only on phones with Gemini Nano (for example Galaxy S25 or Pixel 10). Elsewhere Hopsie works the same, without those features.

What languages do the characters speak?

For now their lines are in Spanish; more languages are on the way.

What if the app crashes?

If Hopsie closes twice in a row, it starts in safe mode: it goes back to your last home screen that worked, without animations, and one tap takes you back to normal mode.
