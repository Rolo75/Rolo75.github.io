Privacy policy

Hopsie Launcher · Last updated: 19 November 2026 · This is a translation; if in doubt, the Spanish version prevails.

Hopsie Launcher is an Android home screen where your app icons are characters that move and react. This page explains what data the app uses and what it does with it. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What we don't do

  • The app has no permission to connect to the internet. It sends nothing off the device.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party services.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone.

Notifications (optional)

If you give notification access, the app knows which app sent you a notification and how many you have unread, so its character can jump or wave. To choose the reaction it may read the notification's title. This is processed on the spot, on your phone.

Only the last notification received (which app, how many and its title) is stored, also on the phone, so the character can react if the home screen was closed in the meantime. No history is kept.

Access is optional and you can remove it whenever you like in Android settings, under Notification access. Without it the app works the same, but the characters don't react to your notifications.

On-device AI features (optional)

On phones with built-in AI (for example, Gemini Nano through Android AICore), Hopsie can use it for three things. Everything happens inside the phone and the app still has no internet connection:

  • Smart bubble: reads the title and text of the last notification to sum it up in one short sentence.
  • Suggested replies: reads the text of the last message to suggest three replies. If you tap one, it's sent with the quick reply offered by the message's own app, just as if you replied from the notification shade. Nothing is ever sent unless you tap a reply.
  • Wallpaper comment: when you change the wallpaper, the AI looks at a small copy of the image so a character can comment on it.

That text and that image are only used at that moment and in memory: they aren't stored, don't go into the app's log and disappear when you read the notification. You can turn off each feature in Settings.

Your apps

To draw the icons, the app checks which launchable apps you have installed. With that list and the order you choose, your pages, the bottom bar and recent apps are saved. Everything is stored on the phone.

Wallpaper

If you choose a wallpaper image, it's done with Android's file picker. The app only stores the reference to that image so it can show it; it doesn't access the rest of your photos.

Sound, vibration and uninstalling

The characters' sound and vibration are saved as settings on the phone. If you use "Uninstall" from an icon's menu, the app asks Android to uninstall that app; Android asks you to confirm first.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app stores from Android settings, under Apps, Hopsie, Storage, Clear data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and doesn't collect personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact
