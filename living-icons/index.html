What is Living Icons?

In Living Icons, icons don't sit still. Each app gets a body that matches what it does (messaging, music, games, camera…), with eyes and arms, and it carries its real icon.

When an app gets a notification, its character jumps and waves. Go back to the home screen and you'll see them wake up; tap them and they react. A battery saver mode keeps the animations from draining your phone.

The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything off your phone. Notification access is optional and explained before it's requested.

What you can do

  • Characters by categoryEach app gets a body based on its type, with its real icon on top.
  • They react to notificationsThey jump and wave when their app gets a notification.
  • A complete home screenPages, dock, app drawer, folders and edit mode.
  • LandscapeThe same pages when you turn your phone sideways.
  • No internetNo network permission: everything stays on the phone.
  • Battery saverFull animations or a saver mode, your choice.

Frequently asked questions

What is Living Icons?

An Android home screen where every icon is an animated character that reacts to its app's notifications.

When will it be out?

The design phase is almost finished. It will start as a closed test on Google Play; if you'd like to try it, write to us.

Does it need internet or collect data?

No. The app has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.

Does it use a lot of battery?

It has a saver mode that tones down the animations. We're measuring it on real phones before release.
