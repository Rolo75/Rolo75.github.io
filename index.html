Independent studio

Home screens that don't look like everyone else's

An independent studio making Android apps. We build home screens that feel different: Aether Tiles, made of live tiles, and Living Icons, where your icons are little characters. No ads, no tracking, and your data stays on your phone.

Our projects

Aether Tiles

Closed testing on Google Play

Aether Tiles is a free Android home screen (launcher) that replaces icons with a grid of live tiles: clock, calendar, battery, weather, music and notifications, with no ads and no tracking.

Living Icons

In development

Living Icons Launcher is an Android home screen where your app icons are animated characters that react when a notification comes in. It works offline, with no ads and no analytics.

See Living Icons →

How we work

We're a small studio. Every app goes to a closed group of testers first and changes based on what they tell us. No ads, no tracking: whatever our apps store stays on your phone.
