Our projects

Aether Tiles

Closed testing on Google Play

Aether Tiles is a free Android home screen (launcher) that replaces icons with a grid of live tiles: clock, calendar, battery, weather, music and notifications, with no ads and no tracking.

See Aether Tiles →

Hopsie

Coming soon to Google Play

Hopsie is a home screen (launcher) for Android where every app is a character with eyes, arms and its usual icon. They react to your notifications, chat with each other and, on phones with built-in AI, suggest replies using the phone's own AI, offline. No ads and no analytics.

How we work

We're a small studio. Every app goes to a closed group of testers first and changes based on what they tell us. No ads, no tracking: whatever our apps store stays on your phone.

Want to try something before anyone else, or have an idea? Write to us at aetherandroid75@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How much do they cost?

Aether Tiles is free with no ads. Hopsie will cost €3.99, paid once. None of them has a subscription.

What data do they collect?

None of them has ads or analytics, and what they store stays on your phone. The details are in each app's privacy policy (links below).

How do I join a test?

Each app's page has the steps and the Google Play button.

Can I go back to my usual home screen?

Yes, whenever you want, without losing anything: Settings > Apps > Default apps > Home app. The exact names vary a little by phone brand.

Which phones do they run on?

Aether Tiles needs Android 10 or later. Hopsie needs Android 8 or later.
