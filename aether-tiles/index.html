What is Aether Tiles?

Aether Tiles replaces your Android home screen with a grid of tiles. Each tile shows live information: the time on the clock face you pick, your next appointment, how much battery is left, the weather in your city or the song that's playing, with buttons to pause it.

The tiles have a glass finish over your wallpaper and a thin line of light that slowly travels around their edges. That light also tells you things: it turns green while charging, red when the battery is low, and spins amber around any app with unread messages.

Everything is stored on your phone. There are no servers of our own, no ads and no analytics. It only goes online for what you turn on: the weather for the city you choose and news tiles.

What you can do

  • Lend modeHand your phone to someone and they only see the apps you choose. Getting out takes your PIN.
  • Notification panelSwipe down on the home screen: notifications grouped by app, music and quick settings.
  • Live tilesClock with eleven faces, calendar, battery, weather, music, notes and news.
  • People as tilesPin your favourite people: one tap and their number is ready to call.
  • Make it yoursFour sizes, plus colour, material, opacity, corners and a background photo for each tile.
  • Widgets and foldersPut your apps' widgets inside a tile and group apps into folders.
  • Privacy modeTiles show how many notifications you have, but not who's writing or what you're listening to.
  • BackupSave your home screen to a file and restore it on another phone.

Frequently asked questions

What is Aether Tiles?

An Android home screen that shows your apps as tiles with live information (time, appointments, battery, weather, music and notifications) instead of static icons.

Is it free? Does it have ads?

It's free, with no ads and no analytics.

Does it collect my data?

No. Everything is stored on the phone. It only goes online for the weather in the city you pick and for any news tiles you add.

What is lend mode?

A way to hand your phone to someone else: they can only open the apps you choose, and leaving it takes your PIN. It isn't parental control.

What permissions does it ask for?

All of them are optional: notifications (counters, light and panel), calendar (next appointment) and usage access (only for lend mode). Without them it works as a regular tile launcher.

How do I go back to my usual home screen?

With one tap from the Aether Tiles settings, or in Android Settings, Default apps.

How can I try it?

It's in closed testing on Google Play. Write to us and we'll add you to the testers group.
